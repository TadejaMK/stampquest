How to Use Stamp Quest

  1. Check today's quest. You get one photo challenge each day.
  2. Open the camera. Your phone's camera fills the screen.
  3. Take a photo. You can also choose a photo from your gallery.
  4. Preview your stamp. The app turns your photo into a collectible stamp.
  5. Save the stamp. It will appear in your collection.
  6. Come back tomorrow. A new daily quest will be ready.

Camera permission is requested only after you tap Open Camera. Your photos are processed locally on your device.
